Our Story

Ashbourne School of Technology was founded in 2001 by a group of visionary educators who believed that quality technology education should be accessible to everyone. What began as a modest training centre with fewer than thirty students has since grown into one of the most respected institutions of its kind on the continent. Our programmes are built around practical, industry relevant learning that prepares graduates to make an immediate impact in their careers.


Over the years, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and data science have reshaped what is possible in every industry on earth. Ashbourne has been at the centre of that shift, equipping students with the tools that are defining the future. The best, without question, is still ahead.

Our Vision

A world where the next breakthrough in technology carries an African signature.

At Ashbourne, we believe Africa is not behind in the technology race. It is simply warming up. We exist to accelerate that moment, to be the institution whose graduates are in the room when history is made, not reading about it the next day.

Our Mission

To build technically excellent graduates who do not wait for the future. They write it.

Every decision we make, from how we design our curriculum to who stands in front of our classrooms, is driven by one question: will this produce someone the world actually needs? We do not manufacture graduates. We develop people who are ready, capable, and hungry enough to go out and make something that matters.

Meet The Team

Behind Ashbourne's walls stands a faculty shaped by decades of rigour, curiosity, and quiet ambition. Our team brings together researchers, engineers, and educators who have carried their craft across some of the world's most demanding institutions, choosing now to devote that expertise to the next generation of thinkers. Each member of staff is selected not only for mastery of their discipline, but for a willingness to mentor with patience and precision. To treat every student's question as worthy of a considered answer. It is this blend of scholarship and stewardship that defines the Ashbourne way: exacting in standards, generous in guidance, and unwavering in the belief that true education is built one relationship at a time.
